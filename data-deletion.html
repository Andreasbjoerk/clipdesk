Clipdesk data deletion

Last updated: 19 November 2026

What Clipdesk stores

Clipdesk stores data only on its owner's own computer, never on a server:

  • the access tokens and account IDs for the owner's connected Instagram accounts and YouTube channels;
  • post data for the owner's own posts: the video files, captions, links, posting times and the statistics read back from the platforms (views, likes, audience by country).

Clipdesk does not collect data about viewers or other users, and nothing is shared with third parties.

How to delete it

  1. Remove Clipdesk's access on Instagram: in the Instagram app, go to Settings → Apps and websites, select Clipdesk and remove it. (If the account is connected through Facebook, also remove it under Facebook → Settings & privacy → Settings → Apps and websites.)
  2. For YouTube, remove Clipdesk at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
  3. To have the data stored on the owner's computer deleted (tokens and post data for an account), email smurfklanse@gmail.com with the account name. The owner deletes it and confirms by email.
