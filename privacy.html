Clipdesk privacy policy

Last updated: 16 November 2026

Clipdesk is a private tool run by its owner on the owner's own computer. It is only used with the owner's own YouTube channels and Instagram accounts.

What it accesses

When the owner logs in a channel, Clipdesk receives access tokens from Google (YouTube Data API: upload, read-only and manage videos). It uses them only to upload the owner's videos, read the channel's name and video statistics, and delete videos the owner chooses to remove.

Storage and sharing

Tokens and statistics are stored only on the owner's computer. Nothing is sold, shared with third parties or used for advertising. Clipdesk does not collect data about viewers or other users.

Clipdesk's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
